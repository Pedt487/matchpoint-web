2. DATA WE PROCESS

  • Email address — one-time-code sign-in, account management (account only)
  • Google account data (name, email, avatar) — optional Google sign-in (Google login only)
  • Display name, optional profile photo — profile, social features (optional)
  • Match data, posts, comments, likes, photos, court info — core features, stats, feed (optional)
  • Push token (device identifier) — notifications (only if enabled)
  • Approximate location at match start — stored as a coarse place name (e.g. "Innsbruck, AT") on the match so you can identify matches in your history. Only with your permission; can be turned off in Settings at any time. No precise coordinates or movement profiles are stored.

The app is fully usable in guest mode without an account or email; matches are then stored only locally on your device.

3. LEGAL BASES

Processing is based on performance of the usage contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, e.g. push, Google login, sharing posts) and our legitimate interest in a secure, functioning service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. PROCESSORS

  • Supabase — hosting, database, authentication and storage (EU data centre, Frankfurt)
  • Resend — delivery of sign-in emails
  • Google — only when signing in with Google
  • Expo — delivery of push notifications

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.

5. RETENTION

We keep your data while your account exists. If you delete your account, the account, profile, posts and photos are removed from our servers. Guest-mode data lives only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall.

6. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. You can delete your account directly in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account.

7. CHILDREN

MatchPoint is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. CHANGES

We update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The current version is always available at this address.
